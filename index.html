How I work

Evidence over assumptions.

The part of cybersecurity I enjoy most is turning an unclear situation into a structured investigation. My projects are deliberately documented so another person can follow the reasoning—not just see the final answer.

In my digital-forensics work, that also meant being comfortable with an inconclusive result. The artefacts I examined did not, by themselves, prove data theft, so I documented that limitation instead of forcing a stronger conclusion.

01 — Build a safe environment Use isolated or simulated scenarios where appropriate so investigations can be performed without touching real malicious content.
02 — Preserve the evidence trail Record indicators, artefacts, screenshots and reasoning so findings remain understandable and auditable.
03 — Separate observation from conclusion Describe what the evidence shows, then state what can—and cannot—reasonably be concluded from it.
